# Ram Chant Journey — Engagement & Retention Design

**Purpose of this doc:** the single place we record *what makes the game
delightful and habit-forming for young children* — and the hard ethical line we
never cross. Update this as we add or change any mechanic that affects how long
a child wants to keep chanting.

> **North star:** the game is engaging because it is *beautiful and meaningful*,
> never because it *manipulates children*. Every mechanic below must pass that test.

Bullseye audience: ages **4–8** (pre / early readers), tolerant up to ~15.
Core purpose: maximize *time-spent-chanting* and build a devotional habit —
ethically, through joy, not compulsion.

---

## 1. The grounding framework (why these mechanics, not others)

We ground engagement in **Self-Determination Theory (SDT)** + **Flow**, the
well-researched drivers of healthy intrinsic motivation in children:

- **Competence** — the child feels capable and sees themselves getting better.
- **Autonomy** — the child is in control; nothing is forced or timed against them.
- **Relatedness** — the child feels connected (to Hanumān, to Rāma, to a parent
  chanting alongside).
- **Flow** — clear goal, instant feedback, difficulty matched to skill, no anxiety.

---

## 2. Mechanics we ARE using (the delight engine)

### 2.1 Rolling / panning background  ⭐ (Venky-loved, KEEP)
The background scene continuously *rolls* as the child chants — Hanumān appears
to rise through a living world. Venky: *"the background image moving makes it
exciting for kids."* This is a **core** mechanic.
- Each "Ram" advances the scroll → immediate, visible cause-and-effect (Competence + Flow).
- The world keeps gently drifting even between chants so it always feels alive.
- Intended refinement: the roll should feel *endless* — it never freezes once the
  art "runs out"; it keeps flowing (via a designed loop or a taller/looping scene
  per stage). Faster chanting → faster, more thrilling roll (the rhythm-speed layer,
  scheduled LAST per the build sequence).

### 2.2 Voice as the only control (embodied chanting)
The child's *own voice saying "Ram"* is what moves Hanumān — the act of devotion
IS the gameplay. No buttons to mash. This fuses the sacred act with the fun.

### 2.3 Instant multi-sensory feedback per chant
Every recognized "Ram" fires, together, within a frame:
- Hanumān hops upward (a satisfying arc).
- A sparkle burst + a rising chime (pitch climbs with progress).
- A glow/aura brightens on the hero.
- The counter ticks up; a happy-face pop.
Instant feedback is the backbone of Flow and of Competence.

### 2.4 Pure "freeze on silence" — never punish
Stop chanting → Hanumān simply pauses. **Nothing is lost. No game-over. He never
falls or fails.** Safety to stop = Autonomy, and removes all anxiety (anti-Flow).

### 2.5 Progress you can feel
- `RAM JAPA` count toward a clear target (e.g. 0 / 11) — a reachable, visible goal.
- `RAM MUDRA` (gold coins) reward the child — collectible delight, tuned later to
  reward *rhythm quality* (not raw count) so it's earned, not grindy.
- Hanumān visibly transforms across the climb (an 11-pose emotional journey:
  prayer → devotion → clarity → confidence → excitement → readiness → flight):
  the child *sees themselves powering the transformation* (Competence).

### 2.6 Meaningful payoff, not a Skinner reward
Stage end: Hanumān grows radiant, the world lights up, he **thanks and blesses**
the child, and voices that stage's teaching (an MBTN Ch.7 line). The reward is
*meaning + beauty*, tied to the story — not a random loot drop.

### 2.7 Beautiful, dignified art (the CoComelon hook, done right)
Premium glowing-storybook chibi art; a dignified Madhwa-tradition Mukhyaprana
(never a cartoon monkey). Kids return to worlds that are gorgeous to be in.

### 2.8 Resumable pilgrimage
Progress saves; longer stages (1008 chants) are designed as multi-day journeys
the child returns to — a *gentle* reason to come back, tied to a real pilgrimage
feeling, not a manufactured streak.

### 2.9 Relatedness: chant with a grown-up
Designed to be joyful to play *with* a parent chanting alongside (deepens the
habit and the bond). Camera-free in v1 (privacy); shared chanting is the social layer.

## 3. Dark patterns we REFUSE (the hard line)

We will **never** use, even if they would boost metrics:
- ❌ Guilt-driven streaks ("you broke your 7-day streak!").
- ❌ Nagging / FOMO push notifications.
- ❌ Grinding, energy timers, or artificial waits.
- ❌ Loot boxes, gacha, or any randomized reward-for-money.
- ❌ Ads or in-app purchases of any kind.
- ❌ Data harvesting / behavioral profiling of children.
- ❌ Shaming leaderboards or social comparison.
- ❌ Anything that makes a child feel *bad* for stopping.

Zero telemetry, no PII, audio never leaves the device.

## 4. Open tuning questions (revisit as we build)
- Endless-roll implementation per stage: seamless-loop art vs. very-tall scene vs.
  procedural parallax layers?
- Rhythm-speed layer (faster chant → faster roll) — scheduled LAST; how much speed-up
  before it stops feeling calm/devotional?
- Coin (Ram Mudra) earning rule once rhythm-quality is in: reward steadiness (CV),
  not raw count.
