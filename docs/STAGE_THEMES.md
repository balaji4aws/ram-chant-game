## Notes
- These are the **theme** titles (the moral/beat of each stage). Each stage ALSO
  has a game/action name in `stages.js` (`title`), e.g. Stage 1's action name is
  "The Great Leap". Per stage we decide whether the big on-screen title shows the
  action-name or the theme-name. Current Stage 1 shows "The Great Leap" as the
  big title + "Say Rāma's name…" tagline; the theme "Start with Rāma" is its
  deeper label (surfaces on the lesson card / stage select later).
- These 7 map to the 7 MBTN Ch. 7 beats (Hanumat-pratiyanam, Srimadhwacharya).
- Lesson cards carry the deeper teaching quote per stage.
