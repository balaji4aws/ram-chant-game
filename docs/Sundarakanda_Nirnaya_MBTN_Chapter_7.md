# Sundarakāṇḍa Nirṇaya

## Mahābhārata Tātparya Nirṇaya --- Adhyāya 7

### Śrī Madhvācārya --- Hanūmatpratiyānam

> **Hariḥ Sarvottamaḥ · Vāyuḥ Jīvottamaḥ**

## About this document

This is a study-friendly compilation of the **50 verses of the seventh
adhyāya of Śrī Madhvācārya's Mahābhārata Tātparya Nirṇaya**, commonly
called **Sundarakāṇḍa Nirṇaya** or **Hanūmatpratiyānam**.

The chapter is the seventh adhyāya of the Mahābhārata Tātparya Nirṇaya
and presents a concise account of Hanumān's mission to Laṅkā, his
meeting with Sītā, his encounter with the Rākṣasas, the burning of
Laṅkā, his return, and Śrī Rāma's response.

The source tradition describes this chapter as a concise rendering of
the Sundarakāṇḍa; one online presentation notes that it condenses the
corresponding narrative into about 50 ślokas.

### How to use this document

Each verse is presented as:

1.  **IAST-style Roman transliteration** --- for readers who cannot read
    Devanāgarī.
2.  **Simple English meaning** --- a study-oriented paraphrase, not a
    word-for-word translation.
3.  **Madhwa learning point** --- a short note highlighting the
    devotional/tattva lesson where useful.

> **Textual note:** Roman transliteration online sometimes differs in
> punctuation, sandhi separation, or minor pāṭha variants. This document
> follows the commonly circulated ITRANS-style text used by the cited
> online editions. For formal pāṭha or publication, the text should be
> checked against a trusted printed Sanskrit edition.

## 7.1

**rāmāya śāśvatasuvistṛtaṣaḍguṇāya sarveśvarāya sukhasāramahārṇavāya \|\
natvā lilaṅghayiṣurarṇavamutpapāta niṣpīḍya taṃ girivaraṃ pavanasya
sūnuḥ \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
The son of Vāyu, Hanumān, bowed to Śrī Rāma --- the Supreme Lord,
endowed with limitless auspicious qualities and immeasurable strength
--- and, pressing down the great mountain with his feet, leapt into the
sky to cross the ocean.

**Learning:** Begin every great task by remembering and surrendering to
Śrī Hari.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.2

**cukṣobha vāridhiranuprayayau ca śīghraṃ yādogaṇaiḥ saha
tadīyabalābhikṛṣṭaḥ \|\
vṛkṣāśca parvatagatāḥ pavanena pūrvaṃ kṣipto'rṇave girirudāgamadasya
hetoḥ \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
The ocean was greatly disturbed by Hanumān's tremendous force. Aquatic
beings and trees seemed to follow him. Mount Maināka, which Vāyu had
earlier placed in the ocean, rose from the waters to help him.

**Learning:** Service offered to a servant of the Lord is itself service
to the Lord.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.3

**syālo harasya giripakṣavināśakāle kṣipto'rṇave sa
marutorvaritātmapakṣaḥ \|\
haimo giriḥ pavana-jasya tu viśramārthamudbhidya
vāridhimavarddhadanekasānuḥ \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Maināka, the golden mountain and relative of Śiva, had been protected by
Vāyu when the wings of the mountains were being destroyed. Now, out of
gratitude to Vāyu, it emerged from the ocean with all its slopes to
offer Hanumān a place to rest.

**Learning:** Gratitude inspires service.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.4

**naivātra viśramaṇamaicchadaviśramo'sau niḥsīm-pauruṣa-guṇasya kutaḥ
śramo'sya \|\
āśliṣya parvatavaraṃ sa dadarśa gacchan devairtu nāgajananīṃ prahitāṃ
vareṇa \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Hanumān, who was never tired and possessed boundless strength, did not
wish to rest. He respectfully embraced Maināka and continued his
journey. Soon he encountered Surasā, the mother of the Nāgas, who had
been sent to test him.

**Learning:** Respect others' goodwill, but remain focused on one's
duty.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.5

**jijñāsubhirnijabalaṃ tava bhakṣametu yadyattvicchasi
tadityamaroditāyāḥ \|\
āsyaṃ praviśya sapadi praviniḥsṛto'smāddevānānandayaduta svṛtameṣu
rakṣan \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Surasā had received a boon that whatever she wished to eat would enter
her mouth. Hanumān entered her mouth and immediately emerged unharmed,
thereby honoring the boon while completing his mission. The gods
rejoiced.

**Learning:** Intelligence and tact can solve a problem better than
force.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.6

**dṛṣṭvā surapraṇayitāṃ balamasya cograṃ devāḥ pratuṣṭuvuramūṃ
sumanobhivṛṣṭyā \|\
tairādṛtaḥ punarasau viyataiva gacchan chāyāgrahaṃ pratidadarśa ca
siṃhikākhyam \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Seeing Hanumān's devotion to the gods and his extraordinary strength,
the gods praised him and showered flowers upon him. Continuing through
the sky, he encountered the demoness Siṃhikā, who could seize a being by
its shadow.

**Learning:** Strength combined with humility earns the respect of the
noble.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.7

**laṅkāvanāya sakalasya ca nigrahe'syāḥ sāmarthyamapratihataṃ pradadau
vidhātā \|\
chāyāmavākṣipad asau pavanātmajasya so'syāḥ śarīramanuviśya bibheda cāśu
\|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Siṃhikā seized Hanumān by his shadow. Hanumān entered her body and
destroyed her swiftly. His power could not be obstructed.

**Learning:** Not every obstacle should be handled with the same method;
discern the situation and act decisively.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.8

**niḥsīmamātmabalamityanudarśayāno hatvaiva tāmapi vidhātṛvarābhiguptām
\|\
lambe sa lambāśikhare nipapāta laṅkāprākārarūpakagirāvatha sañcukocha
\|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Displaying his limitless strength, Hanumān defeated the demoness
protected by Brahmā's boon. He reached the mountain-like fortification
of Laṅkā and then reduced his enormous form.

**Learning:** Great power should be controlled when the situation
requires it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.9

**bhūtvā biḍālasamito niśi tāṃ purīṃ ca prāpsyan dadarśa nijarūpavatīṃ
sa laṅkām \|\
ruddho'nayāśvatha vijitya ca tāṃ svamuṣṭipiṣṭāṃ tayā'numata eva viveśa
laṅkām \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
At night Hanumān assumed a tiny form and approached Laṅkā. Lankinī, the
guardian of the city, obstructed him. He defeated her with a single
blow, after which she allowed him to enter Laṅkā.

**Learning:** Humility and adaptability are strengths.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.10

**mārgamāṇo bahiścāntaḥ so'śokavanikātale \|\
dadarśa śiṃśapāvṛkṣamūlasthitaramākṛtim \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Searching inside and outside the city, Hanumān eventually saw Sītā,
seated beneath a Śiṃśapā tree in the Aśoka grove.

**Learning:** Perseverance eventually reveals the goal.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.11

**naralokaviḍambasya jānan rāmasya hṛdgatam \|\
tasya ceṣṭānusāreṇa kṛtvā ceṣṭāśca saṃvidaḥ \|\
tādṛkceṣṭāsametāyā aṅgulīyamadāttataḥ \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Understanding the divine purpose of Rāma's human-like conduct, Hanumān
carefully followed the appropriate manner of approaching Sītā and gave
her Rāma's ring as the sign of His message.

**Learning:** True service requires understanding the Lord's purpose,
not merely acting impulsively.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.12

**sītāyā yāni caivāsannākṛtestāni sarvaśaḥ \|\
bhūṣaṇāni dvidhā bhūtvā tānyevāsanstathaiva ca \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
The ornaments associated with Sītā's form appeared in the appropriate
manner, confirming her identity and the authenticity of the encounter.

**Learning:** Truth is recognized through appropriate evidence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.13

**atha cūḍāmaṇiṃ divyaṃ dātuṃ rāmāya sā dadau \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Sītā then gave Hanumān her divine crest-jewel to take back to Śrī Rāma
as a token of her identity and message.

**Learning:** A trustworthy messenger carries the Lord's message
faithfully and brings the devotee's message back to Him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.14

**yadyapyetanna paśyanti niśācaragaṇāstu te \|\
dyulokacāriṇaḥ sarvaṃ paśyantyṛṣaya eva ca \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Although the Rākṣasas could not perceive all that was taking place, the
sages and beings who move through the heavenly regions witnessed it.

**Learning:** The absence of ordinary observers does not make a
righteous act unseen.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.15

**teṣāṃ viḍambanāyaiva daityānāṃ vañcanāya ca \|\
paśyatāṃ kalimukhyānāṃ viḍambo'yaṃ kṛto bhavet \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
This apparent concealment was part of the divine arrangement: the events
served to bewilder the hostile beings and reveal the truth to those
capable of seeing it.

**Learning:** Divine actions may have meanings deeper than what appears
outwardly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.16

**kṛtvā kāryamidaṃ sarvaṃ viśaṅkaḥ pavanātmajaḥ \|\
ātmāviṣkaraṇe cittaṃ cakre matimatāṃ varaḥ \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Having completed his confidential mission, Hanumān, the foremost among
the wise, decided that it was now time to reveal himself and make his
presence known.

**Learning:** Wisdom includes knowing when to remain quiet and when to
act openly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.17

**atha vanamakhilaṃ tad rāvaṇasyāvalupya kṣitiruhamimamekaṃ varjayitvāśu
vīraḥ \|\
rajani-caravināśaṃ kāṅkṣamāṇo'tivelaṃ muhuratiravanādī toraṇaṃ cāruroha
\|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Hanumān destroyed the entire Aśoka grove except the tree under which
Sītā sat. Desiring to destroy the forces of Rāvaṇa, he climbed onto the
gateway and waited for them.

**Learning:** Protect what is sacred even while confronting wrongdoing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.18

**athāśṛṇod daśānanaḥ kapīndraceṣṭitaṃ param \|\
dideśa kiṅkarān bahūn kapirnigṛhyatāmiti \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Rāvaṇa heard about Hanumān's actions and ordered a large force of
Kiṅkaras to capture him.

**Learning:** Great opposition often appears when dharma challenges
adharma.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.19

**samastaśo vimṛtyavo varāddharasya kiṅkarāḥ \|\
samāsadanmahābalam surāntarātmanaḥ aṅgajam \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
The Kiṅkaras, who had obtained powerful protections, approached Hanumān,
the immensely powerful servant of Śrī Hari.

**Learning:** Boons and worldly protections cannot overcome the servant
of the Supreme Lord.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.20

**aśītikoṭiyūthapaṃ purassarāṣṭakāyutam \|\
anekahetisaṅkulaṃ kapīndramāvṛṇodbalam \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
A vast army, filled with countless troops, commanders and weapons,
surrounded Hanumān.

**Learning:** Numbers alone do not determine victory.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.21

**samāvṛtastathāyudhaiḥ sa tāḍitaśca tairbhṛśam \|\
cakāra tān samastaśastalaprahāracūrṇitān \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Although surrounded and attacked with many weapons, Hanumān destroyed
the attackers with ease.

**Learning:** Courage comes from strength rooted in devotion and duty.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.22

**punaśca mantriputrān sa rāvaṇapracoditān \|\
mamarda sapta parvataprabhān varābhirakṣitān \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Rāvaṇa next sent seven powerful sons of his ministers, protected by
great boons. Hanumān destroyed them as well.

**Learning:** Repeated obstacles should not weaken determination.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.23

**balāgragāminastathā sa śarvavāksugarvitān \|\
nihatya sarvarakṣasāṃ tṛtīyabhāgam akṣiṇot \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
After defeating the foremost warriors who were proud of their strength,
Hanumān destroyed a substantial portion of Rāvaṇa's fighting force.

**Learning:** Pride in worldly power can quickly collapse.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.24

**anaupamaṃ harerbalaṃ niśamya rākṣasādhipaḥ \|\
kumāramakṣamātmanaḥ samaṃ sutaṃ nyayojayat \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Hearing of Hanumān's incomparable strength, Rāvaṇa sent his son Akṣa,
whom he considered equal to himself in power.

**Learning:** Even great worldly rulers may fail to recognize the true
hierarchy of strength.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.25

**sa sarvalokasākṣiṇaḥ sutaṃ śarairvavarṣa ha \|\
śitairvarāstramantritairna cainamabhyacālayat \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Akṣa attacked Hanumān with sharp arrows empowered by great weapons and
mantras, but he could not move him.

**Learning:** External weapons cannot shake one whose strength is rooted
in the Supreme.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.26

**sa maṇḍamadhyakāsutaṃ samīkṣya rāvaṇopamam \|\
tṛtīya eṣa cāṃśako balasya hītyacintayat \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Seeing Akṣa, who resembled Rāvaṇa in power, Hanumān assessed that Akṣa
represented another major portion of Rāvaṇa's strength.

**Learning:** Hanumān acts with discernment; his actions are deliberate,
not reckless.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.27

**nidhārya eva rāvaṇaḥ sa rāghavasya nānyathā \|\
yadīndrajitmayā hato na cāsya śaktirīkṣyate \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Hanumān understood that Rāvaṇa was ultimately no match for Rāghava. If
Indrajit were to fight after Akṣa's fall, the balance of power would
become even clearer.

**Learning:** A wise servant evaluates the larger mission, not merely
the immediate opponent.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.28

**atastayoḥ samo mayā tṛtīya eṣa hanyate \|\
vicārya caivamāśu taṃ padoḥ pragṛhya pupluve \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Considering Akṣa to be another major share of Rāvaṇa's strength, Hanumān
decided to defeat him and swiftly seized him by the feet.

**Learning:** Thoughtful action is stronger than impulsive action.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.29

**sa cakravad bhramāturaṃ vidhāya rāvaṇātmajam \|\
apothayaddharātale kṣaṇena mārutī tanuḥ \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Hanumān whirled Rāvaṇa's son like a wheel and dashed him to the ground,
defeating him swiftly.

**Learning:** Even overwhelming strength is used by Hanumān only in
service of Śrī Rāma's mission.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.30

**vichūrṇite dharātale nije sute sa rāvaṇaḥ \|\
niśamya śokatāpitaḥ tadagrajaṃ samādiśat \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
When Rāvaṇa heard that his son had been killed, he was overcome with
grief and sent another powerful warrior.

**Learning:** Adharma ultimately brings suffering even to those who
appear powerful.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.31

**athendrajinmahāśarairvarāstrasaṃprayojitaiḥ \|\
tatakṣa vānarottamaṃ na cāśakad vichālane \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Indrajit attacked the foremost of the monkeys with powerful arrows and
great weapons, but he could not move Hanumān.

**Learning:** The strength of the Lord's servant cannot be measured by
ordinary standards.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.32

**athāstramuttamaṃ vidher yuyoja sarvaduḥsaham \|\
sa tena tāḍito harirvyacintayannirākulaḥ \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Indrajit then employed Brahmā's powerful weapon. Hanumān, struck by it,
remained completely calm and reflected on what he should do.

**Learning:** Even in danger, remain calm and think clearly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.33

**mayā varā vilaṅghitā hyanekaśaḥ svayambhuvaḥ \|\
sa mānanīya eva me tato'tra mānayāmyaham \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Hanumān reflected: "I have already overcome many boons granted by
Brahmā. Brahmā, however, deserves my respect. Therefore I shall honor
this weapon."

**Learning:** True greatness includes respect for worthy authorities.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.34

**ime ca kuryuratra kiṃ prahṛṣṭarakṣasāṃ gaṇāḥ \|\
itīha lakṣyameva me sa rāvaṇaśca dṛśyate \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Hanumān thought: "What can these delighted Rākṣasas really do to me? By
allowing this, I will also gain an opportunity to see Rāvaṇa."

**Learning:** A wise person can turn an apparent setback into an
opportunity to complete the larger mission.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.35

**idaṃ samīkṣya baddhavatsthitaṃ kapīndramāśu te \|\
babandhuranyapāśakairjagāma cāstramasya tat \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Seeing Hanumān apparently restrained, the Rākṣasas tied him with
ordinary ropes. Once they did so, the Brahmāstra's binding effect
ceased.

**Learning:** We should understand the difference between genuine
restraint and merely apparent restraint.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.36

**atha pragṛhya taṃ kapiṃ samīpamānayaṃśca te \|\
niśācareśvarasya taṃ sa pṛṣṭavāṃśca rāvaṇaḥ \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
The Rākṣasas brought Hanumān before Rāvaṇa. Rāvaṇa questioned him.

**Learning:** Hanumān's mission had now reached the heart of the enemy's
kingdom.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.37

**kape kuto'si kasya vā kimarthamīdṛśaṃ kṛtam \|\
itīritaḥ sa cāvadatpraṇamya rāmamīśvaram \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Rāvaṇa asked: "O monkey, where have you come from? Whose servant are
you? Why have you done all this?" Hanumān first bowed mentally to his
Lord Rāma and then answered.

**Learning:** Before speaking or acting, remember the Lord.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.38

**avaihi dūtamāgataṃ durantavikramasya mām \|\
raghūttamasya mārutiṃ kulakṣaye taveśvaram \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Hanumān declared: "Know me as the messenger of the immensely powerful
Rāghava. I am Māruti, son of Vāyu, and I am capable of bringing
destruction to your entire lineage."

**Learning:** Hanumān's identity is fundamentally that of a servant and
messenger of Śrī Rāma.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.39

**na cet pradāsyasi tvaranraghūttamapriyāṃ tadā \|\
saputramitrabāndhavo vināśamāśu yāsyasi \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Hanumān warned Rāvaṇa: "If you do not quickly return Rāma's beloved
Sītā, you will face destruction along with your sons, friends and
relatives."

**Learning:** A warning given before a consequence is an act of
compassion when it gives the wrongdoer a chance to correct himself.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.40

**na rāmabāṇadhāraṇe kṣamāḥ sureśvarā api \|\
viriñciśarvapūrvakāḥ kimu tvamalpasārakaḥ \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Hanumān said: "Even great divine beings such as Brahmā and Śiva cannot
withstand the force of Rāma's arrows. How then can you, whose strength
is insignificant before Him, hope to resist?"

**Learning:** In Madhwa tattva, Śrī Hari is Sarvottama --- the Supreme.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.41

**prakopitasya tasya kaḥ purasthitau kṣamo bhavet \|\
surāsuroragādike jagatyacintyakarmaṇaḥ \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Hanumān continued: "Once that Lord of inconceivable deeds becomes
opposed to you, who in the worlds of gods, demons and serpents could
stand before Him?"

**Learning:** Nothing is beyond the power of the Supreme Lord.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.42

**itīrite vadhodyataṃ nyavārayad vibhīṣaṇaḥ \|\
sa pucchadāhakarmaṇi nyayojayanniśācarān \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
When Hanumān spoke these words, Rāvaṇa became ready to kill him.
Vibhīṣaṇa stopped him, reminding him that a messenger should not be
killed. Rāvaṇa therefore ordered that Hanumān's tail be set on fire.

**Learning:** Dharma must be upheld even in the midst of conflict.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.43

**athāsya vastrasañcayaiḥ pidhāya pucchhamagnaye \|\
dadurdadāha nāsya tanmarutsakho hutāśanaḥ \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
The Rākṣasas wrapped Hanumān's tail in cloth and set it on fire. But
Agni, the friend of Vāyu, did not burn Hanumān.

**Learning:** The Lord protects His devoted servant.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.44

**mamarṣa sarvaceṣṭitaṃ sa rakṣasāṃ nirāmayaḥ \|\
baloddhataśca kautukāt pradagdhumeva tāṃ purīm \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Hanumān calmly tolerated everything the Rākṣasas did. Possessing immense
strength, he allowed it because he intended to use the fire to burn
Laṅkā.

**Learning:** Patience is not weakness. Sometimes patience is part of a
larger strategy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.45

**dadāha cākhilaṃ puraṃ svapucchhagena vahninā \|\
kṛtistu viśvakarmaṇo'pyadahyatāsya tejasā \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Hanumān used the fire on his tail to burn the entire city of Laṅkā. Even
the magnificent city built by Viśvakarmā could not withstand Hanumān's
extraordinary power.

**Learning:** The servant empowered by the Lord can accomplish the
seemingly impossible.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.46

**suvarṇaratnakāritāṃ sa rākṣasottamaiḥ saha \|\
pradahya sarvaśaḥ purīṃ mudānvito jagarja ca \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
After burning the splendid city of gold and jewels, together with the
strongholds of the Rākṣasas, Hanumān roared joyfully.

**Learning:** His joy comes from completing the Lord's work, not from
personal pride.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.47

**sa rāvaṇaṃ saputrakaṃ tṛṇopamaṃ vidhāya ca \|\
tayoḥ prapaśyatoḥ puraṃ vidhāya bhasmasād yayau \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Hanumān made Rāvaṇa and his son appear powerless, like blades of grass,
while they watched their city reduced to ashes. He then departed.

**Learning:** Worldly power becomes insignificant before the power of
dharma and the Supreme Lord.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.48

**vilaṅghya cārṇavaṃ punaḥ svajātibhiḥ prapūjitaḥ \|\
prabhakṣya vānarēśiturmadhu prabhuṃ sameyivān \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Hanumān crossed the ocean again. His fellow Vānaras honored him, and
after enjoying the honey of Sugrīva's Madhuvana, he went to meet his
Lord Śrī Rāma.

**Learning:** After completing a great task, return promptly to the Lord
and report faithfully.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.49

**rāmaṃ sureśvaramagaṇyaguṇābhirāmaṃ samprāpya sarvakapivīravaraiḥ
sametaḥ \|\
cūḍāmaṇiṃ pavana-jaḥ padayornidhāya sarvāṅgakaiḥ praṇatimasya cakāra
bhaktyā \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Hanumān approached Śrī Rāma, the Lord of the gods and the embodiment of
countless auspicious qualities, together with the other Vānara leaders.
He placed Sītā's crest-jewel at Rāma's feet and offered full prostration
with devotion.

**Learning:** The culmination of service is offering the result back to
the Lord.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.50

**rāmo'pi nānyadanudātumamuṣya yogyamatyantabhaktiparamasya vilakṣya
kiñcit \|\
svātmapradānamadhikaṃ pavanātmajasya kurvan samāśliṣyadamuṃ
paramābhituṣṭaḥ \|\|**

**Meaning:**\
Śrī Rāma, seeing Hanumān's immeasurable devotion and finding no ordinary
gift worthy of rewarding him, gave something greater --- Himself. He
embraced Hanumān with supreme joy.

**Learning:** This is the emotional climax of the chapter: perfect
service is met by the Supreme Lord's grace.

# 🌺 The central teaching of Sundarakāṇḍa Nirṇaya

The chapter is not merely a story about Hanumān crossing the ocean.

It repeatedly presents a pattern:

**Remember Śrī Hari → understand the mission → use knowledge and
strength → overcome obstacles → remain humble → complete the Lord's work
→ offer the result back to Him.**

For a Madhwa reader, one of the central themes is the relationship
between **Śrī Hari as Sarvottama** and **Vāyu/Hanumān as the supreme
jīva and ideal servant**.

Hanumān's greatness is therefore not presented as independent greatness.
His strength, wisdom and success are directed toward **Śrī Rāma's
service**.

## Editorial note

This document is intended as a **study and learning aid**. The English
meanings are concise paraphrases prepared for readability; they are not
presented as a substitute for a traditional Sanskrit commentary
(*ṭīkā/bhāṣya*).

For children or beginners, a future version could turn each śloka into:

**Śloka → simple meaning → story scene → Madhwa tattva → one question
